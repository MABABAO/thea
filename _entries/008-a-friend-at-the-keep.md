Back at Bandarion Keep.
While researching my budding blacksmith career, I noticed a gap in my training. I am quite proficient with a two-handed mace, but put a smaller one in my hand and apparently I forget how to hit anything.
While applying weightstones, my trainer at the Keep came to mind. Hilda “The Breaker.” I had better arrive prepared.

So I left Undercity armed with a small mace and a shield, taking my usual long detour away from the road. The beasts around here usually leave me alone, but they seem to recognize easy prey. My shield earned its keep. I missed swing after swing before I began to find a proper grip.

At least I gathered some bat and wolf meat along the way. If I am going to keep wandering, I will have to learn to feed myself.

On my way back to the Keep, I realized for the first time that they have a stable, but they don't have an innkeeper, or an inn. I also noticed that they don't have a mailbox either. It was a rather bleak place to call a second home.

Then I spoke to Billmuth.
Billmuth, the deathguard, was beyond pleased. He was genuinely moved by my actions. He had learned that Commander Danitha herself put me on that path, and he didn't look afraid of me anymore. 

He told me that he would personally recount my deeds, and the deeds of our order, to all the Horde and to anyone who would listen to him next time he was in the Undercity. My errand to Sylvanas may not have accomplished what I hoped. But this mattered to Billmuth.

I swear my mace gave a faint glow in that moment. I felt pride and joy. I felt as if I did something meaningful. I have made lots of acquaintances during my trips; I have partnered with people. 

But for the first time, I made a friend.

And I have to come to a decision now.
I have helped as much as possible, and I feel ready to travel beyond these parts. 
In Undercity, I met a rather disturbing demon called Varimathras. He serves the Dark Lady and wanted me to retrieve some special books from Orgrimmar, across the sea in Kalimdor. A very long journey indeed.

Apothecary Johaan also sent me on an errand in Silverpine Forest, southwest of Tirisfal Glades. I feel compelled to see what he wants done there and whether I can further run "interference" on his plans.

There are questions I am leaving behind, too. Why did paladin Breton Samuels call our place Tyr’s Watch? Is that another name for the Keep? Tirisfal. I wonder whether there is a connection.

I have learned that asking the same question over and over will only get me so far. I will have to keep my eyes open and learn what I can along the way.

For now, Silverpine.

Courage and righteousness, Commander Danitha Morr said. That is how I will meet the challenges ahead.
