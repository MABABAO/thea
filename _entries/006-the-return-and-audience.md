I am back in Undercity. Falgon is back with Billmuth.

The Symbol of Life was attuned to him, which made channeling the spell easier than I had feared. And now I know how to call upon Redemption without it. I can do this again.

That should deserve more than a few lines in my notebook. Perhaps I will find the words later. Right now, my audience with Lady Sylvanas keeps intruding.

Commander Danitha had entrusted me with another task: carrying a message of goodwill to Lady Sylvanas. She told me the Forsaken still had their reservations about our order, and that we must meet every challenge with courage and righteousness.

I had seen those reservations in the streets of Undercity. I had hoped things would be different when I stood before its ruler.

Sylvanas outright distrusts us. I concede the point that not all undead paladins manage to adapt to our new condition. I personally had to dispatch some of the paladins that had gone mad; they were killing Forsaken and Scourge alike. But she should know better than to judge all of us by them. She called my fellow paladins a "misbegotten order". She even made a veiled threat that a day may come where she decides "tolerating us" is no longer in the best interests of the Forsaken. According to Commander Danitha, we have proven ourselves again and again that we can be trusted. Well, I trust Commander Danitha. But apparently happiness can also cloud the mind; now I keep returning to something Lord Barthalomew said, something along the lines of "to be accepted back in the order" and Danitha referred to something he said that sounded as if we "could be cured".

Accepted back into the Order. I wish I had asked him what he meant.

Would there be a place for the farmer who shared his kindness with me? For Billmuth, who came asking us to save his friend? Or would I have to leave them behind to be welcomed back?

Perhaps I misunderstood him. I was so happy to be there that I scarcely thought to ask.

But these are my people. They need us.

Every prayer hurts. Still, the Light answers. I don't know why I have been allowed to wield it, or what purpose there is in enduring this pain. But I can heal. I can protect. I brought Falgon back.

Surely those gifts carry a responsibility.

The Light has not abandoned me. How could I abandon the people in my care?
