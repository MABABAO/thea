I know it is wishful thinking but Bilmuth might have been talking. A lot.

When I first arrived at Orgrimmar people there were polite. That's all. Nothing wrong with that but no smiles, no small talk. I went to check a bag vendor, I asked to see what he has and he showed me small bags and medium bags. The latter was 2g. Ouch, that was my entire purse content.

I went for a few days traveling the Barrens, helping people at the Crossroads, went as south as Camp Taurajo. Found good mining nodes of tin and some rare silver nodes, too. I will need them for my new helmet. 

Curiously, after fighting several Quilboars, I found one prisoner at Camp Taurajo. The Tauren let me interrogate him and I found they have politics, too. Who would have thought...

His tribe was at war with the Bristlebacks and asked me to strike at them, in return he would give me important intelligence on the goings of that land. Apparently a few kind words can work wonders. He was not such a bad guy. And the Quilboar have been attacking the Horde since forever. So I struck the deal.

True to the deal, he explained the origin of their magic and who was harassing the Horde. A long time ago there was a cataclysmic war and the Quilboar god, Agamaggan, fell to the earth. The Razorfen Downs and the Razorfen Kraul, made up from the fallen god body is sacred to them and the Quilboar factions have been fighting for control. 

And that he was betrayed by his own people. They left him to die and that's how he became a prisoner to the Horde. I couldn't bring him to tell me why he was betrayed.

What a sad story. After all that, I think the Downs warrant some investigation.

But I am digressing. When I came back to Orgrimmar, I was welcomed at the gate! A grunt told me "welcome, paladin"! OK, that was a good change! 

Now I have more than 4g in my purse, my travels to the Barrens were very lucrative. I went back to that bag vendor, he smiled at me and showed me his collection. He told me the medium bag cost 1,90g! Ho! They're all being friendly is one thing, but I even got a discount!  When I asked about the price he told me this was the correct price for an honored customer. Me! Honored!

Thea Lightbearer, Honored Champion of the Horde!

Back to earth.
I am becoming restless to go back to our Keep. Right after my helmet.
