I did it. Old Fire-Eye is defeated.

I know I should feel triumphant, and I am. But that was also a moment of introspection. I saw the whole fight before it really happened, I knew what I should do at every step, literally. There was no other possible outcome, the conviction I had in the Light and in my training reduced the entire experience to one focal point, right then and there. I saw nothing else, thought about nothing else.

I could use the Hearthstone to bring me back to the Sepulcher, but I chose to walk. And think.

I had spent so much time thinking about how to defeat Old Fire-Eye that I had hardly thought about what would come afterward.

Banon was still dead. Lumina had lost her friend, and nothing I had done could change that.

Still, there would be no more travelers falling prey to that beast. No more people suffering what Banon had suffered.

I suppose that was worth considerably more than proving I could defeat it.

When I finally arrived at the Sepulcher, Lumina looked anxious. "Is it done?" She looked me up and down, looking for signs of a fight. She might not know we Undead are not affected by the Worgen Curse as the living are. Now I started to think I had never considered how that would affect an Undead paladin, honestly.

After my account, she was delighted to know the beast never landed a claw or a fang on me, and she became introspective herself.

Her thoughts surfaced when she mentioned that Banon may now rest in peace. She was new to shamanism herself, and especially attuned to the element of Air, but was new to the other elements. Banon and his fellow shamans were teaching Lumina all they could about our material plane of existence and its relations with the other elements, while learning quite a lot about Air themselves.

Their mission was as much about diplomacy as it was about mutual discovery. Now I could better understand how deeply affected Lumina really was by her loss.

As she approached to examine the blade, a smile surprisingly cracked her sad face. "Banon would have loved to see this."

Seeing my curious expression, she continued. "The material world is an expression of balance between the elements. This blade has its nature anchored in Fire, Earth, Water and now Life, thanks to the slaying of that beast."

"It is lacking something from my home, Air. I believe that I can impart some magic upon your freshly blooded blade to infuse it with a small measure of the magic of my people."

Lumina's eyes began to glow a vibrant blue as she touched the bloodstained blade in my hands.

Instantly the blade glowed with the same blue glow as her eyes, and I felt the transformation. My sense of the Light was stronger, but at the same time tranquil, tamed.

"It is a small thing, I could scarcely improve such a magnificent blade. Regardless, I will pray to the winds that it serves you well, my dear paladin."
