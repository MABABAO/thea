Anyways, I saw enough. I needed a competent party to infiltrate the place. I made camp in the nearby crossroads and started making dinner. It worked as I planned, several passers sat near the fire and everyone pitched in their crafts to make our camp as cozy as possible, with a tent and first aid supplies. I contributed with a grinding wheel if anyone had to sharpen their weapons.

4 of them told me they were looking for a way inside the Keep, each with different objectives. That place was packed with magical stuff and Arugal himself had amassed quite a collection. 

When we were rested we decided to take our chances. A priest, a warrior, a mage and a warlock joined me. The warrior would take point while I was to dish damage and protect our cloth members. 

As advertised those were the true horrors of Silverpine Forest. Worgs, worgen, ghosts, scourge undead and Alliance looking soldiers. What was Arugal planning? 

We carefully made our way while we checked for clues for each party member's objectives. They seemed quite the professionals. I surely did look the part, too.

We found a worgen spy that helped us open the main door to the patio, a nice shortcut that avoided most of the guards. I wanted to question him but he was careful to make himself scarce and not be found entangled with us.

And so we confronted Arugal. Long story short, we defeated him. Short story long, I doubt we have heard the last of him. He cast teleport and illusions, moved around the room as we chased him. I got the disturbing feeling we were somehow played. How can we be sure we killed him and not an illusion? He seemed dead. As I said, just a feeling.

Besides, my Enchanted Silver ingots were right there over his stable, as well as other assorted items that my team helped themselves with. I took what I needed and head out.

We got out by the fastest way but the mage stayed behind, he wanted to rummage the books. Risky, but mages have ways to get in and out, I didn't doubt his abilities. 

Back in the Sepulcher, Dalar and Allister heard my accounting of what transpired, both inside the Keep and at Pyrewood Village.

Dalar and Allister concluded that the shackles I had gathered were indeed used to imprison the populace, but their magic was nowhere near powerful enough to create the worgen.

They seemed relieved. Apparently, Arugal wasn't as powerful as they had feared. But if he wasn't responsible, who was? And what was he planning?

Something else was at play and I was sent to the great Graymane Wall south of Pyrewood to search for clues.

But if I was to confront worgen head on, I needed my Wolfsbane.
