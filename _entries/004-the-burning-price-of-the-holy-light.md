The last few days have been a rush of exhausting labor. Brill and the surrounding countryside are desperately short-handed, so I have thrown myself into helping the Deathguards, the townsfolk, and even the Royal Apothecary Society - though I watch those plague-weavers like a hawk now. I read every document they hand me. I will never be tricked into committing an atrocity again. (Though I must admit, those strange murlocs by the coast are quite funny creatures).

My hard work has paid off. I've amassed a small fortune! Well, perhaps two gold pieces once I count all the silver, but to me, it feels like a king's ransom.

Almost every copper of it, however, went straight to the paladin trainer to deepen my bond with the Light. Learning the spells feels less like studying and more like unearthing buried treasure; the magic is already inside me, waiting to be remembered. But the part I never anticipated - the part no memory prepared me for - is the agony.

Channeling the Light through a dead vessel hurts, especially calling upon the second rank of Flash of Light. It hurts far worse than maintaining a Devotion Aura or manifesting a Blessing of Might. And the healing is never fully whole—the wounds close, leaving scars, but my skin keeps its greyish hue. I suppose I had hoped for something more.

But nothing compares to the Seals and Judgment.
The moment I invoke a Seal, the iron handle of my mace transforms into a white-hot rod. The pain doesn't stop at my hands; it burns my entire body. Judgment feels like an explosion inside me. I tried fighting a few rabid beasts without using the Seals to spare myself the torture, but I was instantly overpowered. The holy magic is mandatory. I have to endure it.

Strangely, eating wild mushrooms, cheese, and drinking cold milk helps soothe the internal burns afterward. It is hilarious to me that a corpse still needs to eat and drink to recover, but I won't complain about the comfort of it.

There is one final anomaly I cannot explain: the pain feels different when I am fighting the Scarlet Crusade, different enough to get my attention. When my mace strikes a zealot, it still hurts but the holy fire burning my hands feels somewhat quieter. I don't know why that is, but I intend to find out. 

My strength has grown considerably. I feel the holy energy thrumming beneath my cold skin, and I believe I am finally ready to leave Brill. I am going to travel northwest, toward a place one trainer called our "second home." I have no idea what that means, but it sounds promising and it is a long, hazardous journey.
I leave at sunrise. For now, I am absolutely dying for a good night's sleep.
...I learned that joke in the Undercity. I think it's growing on me.
