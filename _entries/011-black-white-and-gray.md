With all its wonders, Orgrimmar lacks paladin facilities. If I ever want to retire, I might open a chapter there.

I have the equivalent of two gold coins in my purse, now. I am diligently auctioning the good stuff I get in my journeys, but I am reserving the gems I found during mining operations. I used some of them to craft my boots, so I expect I will need them in the near future, the higher I scale up my skills. I have a new powerful two-handed mace, looks beautiful with green hues; more importantly, it hits hard.

Travelling back and forth to the Undercity has become second nature. And I was curious about Varimathras' request.
The Legion. I have no idea what this means.
Nether. No idea what it is, either.

The Undercity has lots of books. Orgrimmar is no different. I better start reading whenever the opportunity presents. So much I don´t know.

The books Varimathras ordered have ominous names, but he told me that the Dark Lady wants them to overthrow the Lich King and completely free the Scourge. I can stand by those objectives.

Less optimistic is the Royal Apothecary Society state of affairs. Apothecary Renferrel at the Sepulcher told me with so many words that they are under extreme pressure from the Dark Lady to develop a New Plague, and he wants me to deliver a letter to another Apothecary. I heard him. And I didn't like it one bit.

After I delivered the books to Varimathras, I noticed a paladin near the throne. He was calling me and told me he wants to be sure Sylvanas knows we paladins are supporting her, and told me to go to the old ruins of Lordaeron where there is still some Scourge activity.

And since I accepted it, I took a few other requests for that dangerous place. Long story short, I partnered with four people, found lots of good equipment there, and I have an equivalent of 4 gold now.

I saw Scourge zealots there, and they look very much like the apothecaries in the Undercity.

I don't know what to believe. I was a paladin in life, and even in death I am still drawn to the Light. What if some of the Forsaken were drawn to the Scourge before they died? Would freedom from the Lich King really change what they believed?

So, Thrall enlisted warlocks although some of them might want to destroy the Horde from within.

Sylvanas accepted paladins with reservations.

Maybe she has done some deal with the apothecaries. Varimathras is a Dreadlord Demon, whatever that means, and he seems legitimately intent on overthrowing the Lich King, in the name of Sylvanas.

I am learning that knowing whom someone serves tells me very little about them.

Maybe it is time to explore some more south of Orgrimmar. I like the sunny climate and open horizons.
