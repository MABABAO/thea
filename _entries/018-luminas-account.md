Back at the Sepulcher, Lumina told us what happened. 

"You should know that it wasn't actually the Gnolls who attacked us on the road. We were actually attacked by a giant, wolf-like creature that I now know as a worgen." 

"It dispatched our guards and came for me, but Banon threw himself in its path. I managed to get a spell off which startled the creature, causing it to flee, but Banon was already badly bitten. He insisted I leave him there but I refused. We were limping back here when the gnolls found us. What came next was terrifying..."

"I've never seen anything like what happened to Banon. Azeroth is a wonderful place, but the sheer breadth of what is possible here is truly terrifying."

"So now you understand. At the very end of his strength after being pierced by a dozen gnoll blades, he reared up to strike me. In that final moment we locked eyes, and I could tell he still knew who he was and that he recognized me. He knew what was happening but he had no control over himself. That was the worst part."

She learned Trevan sent me along the trail to save her. That was good because she, too, have heard "less than charitable things about paladins". Yet again, Commander Danitha Morr's wisdom was true. This was as much about reputation as it was honoring the Light.

I asked Trevan to send word to the order. Our mission wouldn´t be completed without returning Banon's body to the Earthen Ring. They might have rituals for those sort of things.  

Here I am making friends again, now among the Windshapers, a faction of High Elves that sided with the Horde. And she, too, is fighting prejudice. The Earthen Ring might have had good reasons to embrace the Shen'dorei, but half the time a Horde warrior was struck by an arrow, it came from a Quel'dorei bow. Few care to make the distinction.
