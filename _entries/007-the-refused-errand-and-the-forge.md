Apothecary Johaan sent me on another errand, this time to feed something to a captured Alliance mountaineer. I took the "quest" and the tainted thing with me, and when I got far enough, I destroyed it. Simply refusing wouldn't do. I also had to destroy the means.

Now I wonder how many other paladins have accepted such errands and done exactly what I did. Surely I am not the only one who would refuse.

If word of this “little mutiny” reaches Sylvanas, what then? If she has a personal interest in the apothecaries’ work, our order could find itself standing directly in her way. Creating more Forsaken? More undead? Is that what she wants?

Lots of ifs, I know.

Existential worries should wait. I need armor.

There is a reason I take such long detours between places. Copper mostly turns up among the rocky outcrops, so I keep away from the road and watch for its telltale glint. Even at a brisk walk, I stop often. According to the blacksmiths in Undercity, I will need a lot of it.

I sold all my herbs at the Auction House and paid a journeyman blacksmith to train me. I had already found some good equipment during my travels, but now I have made a breastplate, a belt, and mail wristguards myself.

The boots surprised me most. I had to spend a few more silver on ingredients I was missing, but the moment I put them on, I felt a surge of power. A faint blue light shimmered around them. And they fitted perfectly.

I won't have to depend entirely on what people are willing to give me. I can make things of my own.
