Thank the Light I met a priest that was on a pious quest to recover a body. She looked fierce and capable if she managed to get that close to the Gnoll keep.

We agreed to help each other. And if it wasn't for that lucky encounter, I would have been in serious problems. She literally kept me alive during the entire ordeal. I speculated using a shield, but my armor really looked up to the task and I thought it would be wiser to dispatch the opposition faster with my Fallen blade. My parrying technique with a two-handed sword might give me some needed defensive maneuvers and partially compensate for the lack of a shield. Thank you Hilda. 

I had the serendipity to start looking from their basement and struck luck. A blue slender body was curling against the wall when I finally cleared the path to the jail room. She recognized me as a Forsaken and immediately stood up.

I was a little reticent about freeing an elf and asked her if she was Alliance. "Quel'dorei? No, I am no "quel'dorei". I am Lumina Windsinger and I am shen'dorei. One of the Windshaper Skyborne. I came to these lands as a guest of the Earthen Ring. My companion Banon and I were passing through Silverpine to reach the Arathi Highlands so that we could study the elemental convergences there. Clearly, we didn't make it. We have no time for this now though. Kill the gnolls nearby. Retrieve the key to this cage and free me. I will tell you the rest once we are safely away." 

Shen'dorei. Windshaper Skyborne. I had to go on with my instincts on that, I wish my contacts would have me better prepared for that encounter.

And I will write a complaint letter to the Gnoll chief-in-command. They have to standardize their uniform, without any means to differentiate between them I had to look for the jailer everywhere! 

And all the while, helping Elowen to find the body she was charged to provide a honorable bury. 

Which we did, before I could find the damn key. We took our time to place his remains in an urn and hide the urn in a secret alcove inside the Keep. Yes, she was talking to the dead body and getting instructions. The moment we managed the did, her entire body illuminated, like she was casting Holy Light in herself. That's why you never doubt the Light, folks.

When I witnessed it, I braced myself for the expected burning, but the light felt more like a gentle bathing. I really hope one day to experience what sister Elowen did, her face was of pure calm and peace.

Anyway, eventually I found the key and freed the Lumina. I could tell the Gnoll were not happy. 

"Now that I'm free I'm going to make these vile creatures regret ever laying eyes on a Windshaper."

Wave after wave of Gnoll tried to stop us, but Lumina was not a slack, she fought as much as I while Elowen kept healing us. Once we had 6 Gnoll upon us, I panicked just a little bit, but then started hitting one after the other, to keep their attention on me, while Consecrating the ground around me. Just like I saw the guys doing at the Chasm and the Old Ruins. Easy. 

OK, not easy, but we got out of the Fenris Keep alive! 
The Sister Elowen told me she had to report back to her superiors and left us. May the Light bless her in all her endeavors.
