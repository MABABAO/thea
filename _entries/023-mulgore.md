I thought The Barrens was beautiful, but Mulgore is an absolute eye candy!

The rolling plains, the crown of hills surrounding the entire region, and there's Thunder Bluff.
It is not as grandiose as Orgrimar, which is heavy on stone masonry. Instead it is much soft in the eye.
It is also a thousand meters from the ground! Or maybe not but it looks so up high in the sky when I watched from below.

Cairn Bloodhoof himself greeted me! The chief of the Tauren! He welcomed me, told me any warrior of the Horde has a place in Thunder Bluff. When I remember my one and only talk with Sylvanas...

The Tauren there are really big, their maces and axes might weight more than me, armor included!

They are so deeply spiritual, with their druids and shamans and all. They keep talking about strength and honor. Some nee help with mundane tasks, some need a strong arm. But several are trying to heal the land, to understand the forces of nature, to study it instead of just fighting beasts. 

If there's anyone that can be closer to the Light, it might be a Tauren. 

Anyways, I have been training a lot, doing bids for the Horde in Durotar, the Barrens, Mulgore, and I went as far as Stonetalon Mountains. I fought Quilboars, renegade Tauren who appear to have no honor whatsoever. Those Tauren in question razed entire villages and dabble in strange magic. I won't put much thought in their magic, I have learned how little I know about those things, but razing villages is definitely on my no-no list. And they were in league with a bunch, Venture Co. or something, that was engaged in a violent deforestation of entire sections of the mountains.

I saved a Tauren shaman from her prison. She doesn't know what they wanted with her but her immediate thought was to send word to their friends and family she was OK. And what she wanted from me? To collect special seeds that she could imbue with magic powers to regrow the forest. She didn't want revenge, she wanted to save the land. Damn, you gotta love those Tauren.

When I got back to Thunder Bluff I felt strong enough and planned to go back to the Undercity for more paladin training. Lo and behold, the Tauren do host a paladin trainer in their capital! Cmon! Orgrimmar doesn't have but Thunder Bluff does?! 

Of course we found the only gloomy place in that entire marvelous region to place an undead paladin trainer.  Oh well.

Time to test if I am strong to face the horrors of Silverpine. I have been so infatuated with the beauty around me, I confess I am a little bit ashamed, if there's a place in need of a paladin, it is Silverpine Forest. There I should go now.
