Apparently an ex Dalaran mage called Arugal is responsible for the worgen plaguing Silverpine Forest.
As always, nothing is as simple as it seems. 

According to plan, I went scouting the vicinity of Shadowfang Keep. 

Close by in its shadow is Pyrewood Village. 

Strangely, the whole village was deserted, no one to be seen. Not a single beast, scourge, wolf, worgen, nothing. The inn was well stocked, the fires were ablaze, it would be cozy if it wasn't eerily empty. Stables with horses but no one attending to them, the blacksmith in order, everything in order if not for the complete silence around me. Are they all ghosts and I cannot see them? 

I sat for some time to think things over. I visited every building of the little town. Twice.

Suddenly near sundown they started to appear everywhere. They looked like humans, Alliance clad.

And as suddenly as they started to appear, they attacked me furiously. 

All my training clicked in place, looking for high ground, establishing perimeter, looking around to assess immediate threats. 

After some minutes of hard battle an Orc warrior and an undead warlock came thru the village gates and immediately joined the fray. "We are here for the book, too. Priest Allister sent us and told we might encounter allies for the quest".

I had no idea what book they were talking about, but was glad for the help. "I was sent by Dalar Dawnweaver to investigate how the worgen infestation seem to spread. He thinks it has something to do with shackles some corpses have".

They were glad to help me, although they seemed clueless as myself when they heard me. 

I was the better armored of the trio, so I took point. 

My latest training focused on using my two-handed sword to both parry enemy blows and thrust moves for some powerful strikes when the opening appeared. That's why of late I am not using my shield and mace when tanking foes. And the training paid off. Sometimes I let the warrior take the lead, I healed myself and him, then pressed forward some more. 

We carved our path to a place where some high ranking people seemed to live and there my new partners found their book. 

During all the fights I was checking the fallen soldiers, most of them had shackles. Why would normal people have shackles in their ankles? I placed several of those shackles in my backpack as we started to make our way out of the village, with the same furious fights as when we were on our way in.

We parted ways and I decided to take a peek inside the Keep, prepared to cast protection and run at the first sight of peril. 

The scene I witnessed right near the entrance was shocking. I saw with my own eyes a mage killing a worgen. I was taken by surprise because he himself was surrounded by worgen, clearly on his side! The following exchange was fast and confusing. The mage called himself Arugal, and he was trying to force the subdued worgen into his service, but that fallen worgen still tried to fight them. He dressed differently than the others, too. 

Two factions of worgen? Why not, it will never be straight and simple as I want things to be, right?
