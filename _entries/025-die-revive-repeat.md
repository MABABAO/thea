I died three times.
But the most annoying part was to spend 20 silver to fix my expensive gear.
Or maybe to know I have died in vain.
Or that my latest paladin training cost me more than 2 gold! I will show them "Novice".
Or that I have no idea ho to reach the Deadmines in Westfall. 

OK, deep breaths.
I made my way to Booty Bay, at the southernmost tip of the Eastern Kingdoms.
I was chatting with some adventurers and I have learned two things, one, there's a Horde camp near the northern part of the Stranglethorn Vale, two I would have to learn to ghost walk and leapfrog. 

It would greatly save money to reach the Horde camp. Why? Because reaching the Deadmines includes traveling thru Stranglethorn Vale and that it would be great to have a flight point to there, making my journey shorter.

I remember well Ashenvale, having to stick to the very middle of the beaten road.
STV is worse, the animals are both more powerful and even more savage, if they spot you from afar, they would chase you. And they chased me, a lot. 

I got near the Horde camp but there were no roads, I had to cross the dense jungle. Then an enormous gorilla jumped me and I died.

I came back at the not so near Spirit Healer (why do they call it this, anyway?) and ghostwalked to my corpse.
Yeah. Ghostwalk is a word. And it is self-explanatory, too. 

Quick check, Light inventory, all there, Devotion Aura and for good measure Blessing of Kings. Not that helped much, right upon bringing myself up, a red tiger attacked me, I chose unwisely a direction where other tigers were waiting. 

Dead. Spirit Healer (same one). Ghostwalk. Recover my corpse. Alive. Heal. Aura. 

This time I chose to bring myself to life over an outcrop that I could see a path to the camp, not far away. Three Raptors over me, I almost made to camp.

Dead. Spirit Healer (same one). Ghostwalk. Recover my corpse. Alive. Heal. Aura. 

Now I made a run for it, I used one mana potion and one Lay on Hand blessing, barely made it while strange plants started spitting venom or acid or both on me. Even the plants attack you in this land!!!

But finally I arrived at... Grom'gol. Yeah. The same one I could have reached by Zepellin from Orgrimar.
As I said before, that ghostwalk leapfrog cost me 20 silver on gear repairs after being mangled by almost everything I laid my eyes upon. 

It was clear to some guys there that I was trying to cross STV. They said me to stock up protection scrolls, get some druid and priest buffs and blessings and also some movement speed potions. Plural. 

Apparently there's some invisible threashold that, if crossed, would allow me to reach the next Spirit Healer and revive nearer Westfall. 

The things we learn on this life.

So I took one zeppelin to the Undercity and stocked on everything I needed.
I was rich. Now, after spending 2 gold on training and one more gold on potions, I am back to 4 gold. Up side, I still have 4g.

I decided to test my training and skills in Silverpine.

I can say that the mages of Dalaran will not be casting their fireballs upon unsuspecting passersby for a long time. At one time, bored of chasing one by one, I jumped from a cliff into a house roof and from there in the middle of a table four mages were gathered around, no doubt plotting next attacks. I dispatched everyone.

Next test, attack an enclave of scourge ghouls and armored skeletons. 

I needed to be less harsh, but I was as effective at them, too. 

Equipment, training and persistence.

I saw from afar the outskirts of a gloomy village and the silhouette of what appeared to be Shadowfang Keep, if the road signs were correct. 

Next, scout my way around it.
