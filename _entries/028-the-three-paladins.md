After much work, fighting, and awkwardness, I finally arrived at the Deadmines, deep inside the Alliance territory of Westfall.

The Alliance presence wasn't much of a hassle, mostly because the locals in Moonbrook were trouble enough for Alliance and Horde alike. An entire village working for thugs, pirates, and thieves—who would have thought?

Luck was on my side again. I ran into two other paladins on the exact same quest to forge the Wolfsbane: Cerlea and Ironjaw. One of them had already found the secret entrance to the mines hidden inside a massive barn.

The Deadmines weren't quite the labyrinth that the Wailing Caverns were, though we still spent plenty of time running back and forth before finding the entrance to the pirate's den.

Once the fighting started, we had a blast. We were a commando squad wielding the Light. By splitting our auras, seals, and blessings among the three of us, we became an unstoppable unit. Cerlea drew the enemy's attention, Ironjaw focused his strikes on whatever she engaged, and I played fallback healer—judging one seal at a time while saving my mana to keep us alive.

Inside the pirate's den, it didn't take long to find the goblins carving masts for their shipyard. They fought hard against our combined assault, but to no avail. We claimed our pieces of Whitestone Oak Lumber and cleared the room.

We built a bonfire right there to discuss our next steps. (Yes, I know—a bonfire inside a wood-carving workshop.)

Sitting around the flames, we compared notes on our progress. None of us knew how much lumber to take since Trevan’s notes didn't specify a weight, and the oak was heavy enough that I couldn't haul extra for future paladins. Since the lumber was my final component, I spent some time giving Cerlea and Ironjaw tips on how to track down the Kor Gem.

We also talked about how easily we had carved through the mines, and what a full five-paladin squad could accomplish. I haven't been to the true frontlines yet—just close enough while traversing Ashenvale—so I wondered aloud if the Forsaken or the Horde even fielded full paladin squads. Judging by our run, a unit like that could wreak absolute havoc.

Ultimately, we decided not to push deeper into the mines. Our objective was in hand, and there was no reason to linger.

As I prepared to leave, I realized the materials themselves might not even be the most important part of this quest. Sure, we needed them to forge the blade. Yet retrieving the Kor Gem had taught me humility, surviving the Arugal affair gave me my first taste of covert infiltration behind enemy lines, and here I was, basking in the shared strength of fellow paladins.

Thankfully, I had planned ahead and bound my Hearthstone directly to the Sepulcher. With a flash of light, I was home. The Wolfsbane was finally within reach—I just hoped Trevan had made progress on his end of the research.
