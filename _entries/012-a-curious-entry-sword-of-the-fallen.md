The moment I touched it I felt a surge in both strength and something that seemed attuned to the Light. And it felt right in my hands, even better than my mace.

As with the small mace, I had to learn a proper grip. Strangely, the two-handed sword took less time. They are entirely different weapons; perhaps I shouldn't compare them. But that is how it felt.
Soon I was striking sure and straight. Nobody seemed to notice I was a novice with it, either.

Not that saved it from my mishandling, though.

First thing after coming back from the ruins was to have it properly serviced by a blacksmith.

"Hello! You got yourself one of them Sword of the Fallen! Old lore has it they were popular among Alliance paladins. I will charge you a few silver coins for the service, you will notice how well preserved that blade is, I didn't have much to do.
Now, lets see if i can teach you something about sharpening stones".
