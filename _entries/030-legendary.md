I knew what I must do. Hunt down Old Fire-Eye and kill it with the blade purposefully built to fight the Worgen that defied death itself.

I had the map of Silverpine Forest in my mind. There was only one place I hadn't scouted out enough. At that time I thought it was only an old ruin. Infested with worg wolves and Worgen. This had to be the place.

I approached the place carefully, just like I saw hunters doing it, advancing purposefully while looking for signs of something not quite in place.

I was beginning to doubt my wisdom when I noticed, near a sheer wall next to the ruins, a purple-violet glow moving in the shadows.

There it was. Lumina never mentioned how huge it was, or I wasn't paying attention to her account. Now I was paying attention, alright.

As I slowly released the blade from my back, I felt something stir inside me. It was a mix of sharpness of mind and purpose. I know better than to attribute to the Light things that happen inside me, but it was as if I were being drawn to the beast with a sense of purpose.

That feeling arose almost as a conviction, as I rehearsed in my mind the opening sequence of the fight to come. The seal blessing the blade, the judgement and the following Holy Strike. The consecration of the earth we were walking on, engulfing both beast and foe. I was burning inside as if I had already cast those spells.

Then I struck.

At each strike that glow seemed to flicker and weaken, but he wasn't touched.

After a few more strikes the glow was gone and its protection was finally broken.

I pressed the attack, testing which seals would be most effective as it became increasingly erratic in its attacks, my strikes finally landing.

I baited it into attacking me while I was inching toward that sheer wall. One more round of Consecration and a Holy Strike, and it was cornered. The last judgement finished the beast once and for all.

The blade kept its promise, Banon from the Earthen Ring was avenged, his sacrifice made true, and Lumina had her revenge.
