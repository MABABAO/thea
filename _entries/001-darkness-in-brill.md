Writing might help put my thoughts in order. I can only hope, because my mind is an absolute blur.

I am sitting on a creaking bed in the village of Brill. Gallows' End tavern. The room is cold, smelling faintly of damp earth and rot, but it is oddly cozy. My very first purchase after wandering into town was this notebook. I needed it. The last few days have felt like drowning in a thick, grey fog - doing things without knowing why, walking with a hollow chest, yet constantly yearning for something I couldn't name.

But tonight, the fog somewhat parted. And a title came back to me.

Thea Lightbearer. Paladin of the Order of the Silver Hand.

How do I know those words? I have no memory of learning them, no recollection of ever kneeling to take an oath. When that realization came is the important part, though. It brought a wave of guilt. Because today, I did something despicable.

I cannot sleep. Every time I close my eyes, I see that poor Scarlet zealot.

Damn that Apothecary Johaan. Earlier in the day, the world felt simpler. I had helped an undead farmer harvest pumpkins from his field. He was kind, strangely gentle for a corpse, and despite the roaming Scarlet Crusaders making the work troublesome, helping him gave me a spark of genuine warmth. I felt good. But then I met Johaan. He told me I needed to learn "the ways of the Forsaken." He commanded me to take some pumpkins and feed one to a captured Scarlet prisoner. To serve the Dark Lady, he claimed.
I did as I was told. And I watched as the prisoner twisted, screamed, and rose as a ghoul.
That was when the words came back to me: Paladin of the Order of the Silver Hand. Nothing else followed. But I knew I should never have done this. 

Fighting the Scourge or the Crusade in battle is one thing, but turning the living into the undead?

I refuse to believe Lady Sylvanas commands this. She couldn't possibly intend to make monsters of the living. I was saved by the Light. I see good Forsaken all around me - people who still cling desperately to the echoes of their previous lives, who miss their families, who feel kindness. There must simply be a divide. There are good Forsaken, and there are cruel ones. Sylvanas cannot be one of the bad ones.
From this moment on, I will not blindly follow orders. I will read every instruction. I will question every demand. I need to keep this diary just to look back on what I miss from one day to the next, to keep my own soul awake.
