I tell you, it was hard and I was lucky. And I didn't even start to work on Wolfsbane.

The moment I arrived at Bandarion Keep I was urged to talk to Commander Danitha straight away. I must have been expected because I saw someone beckoning me from high up the Keep.

I keep saying Bandarion Keep but all around me people is saying Tyr's Watch. I will get to that, one day.

I can see why the urgency. Commander Morr explained me a pair of dignitaries from the Earthen Ring went missing in Silverpine Forest en route to Tarren Mill. We didn't know much else but rumors went that this has become quite the diplomatic incident. If we can recover these missing persons, it could help gain us the legitimacy we so dearly need. I was to meet with a fellow paladin at The Sepulcher, Trevan Rol. 

The missing diplomats were a Tauren and an Elf! An  Elf! I have heard of the Earthen Ring but what an elf was doing with us?

I had postponed my Fenris Keep missions since I was stonewalled. I don't have that luxury anymore.

Why Fenris Keep? Upon confering with my contact, Trevan Rol and the Deathguard Baldren, they arrived at the assumption that the large body our scouts have spoted the Gnols dumping in a pyre in Fenris Isle looked very much like a Tauren body. The fact nobody noticed a slender smaller body gave me hope. Baldren sneared at my paladinistic optimism.

I know paladinistic is not a word, but I live by its meaning. Commander Morr bestowed upon me this task and by the Light I will see it thru.

Arriving at the outskirts of the fortress was easy. Finding the huge mangled Tauren body was also easy. The Tauren body seem to be savaged attacked. I know what a sword can do and that was not all I was seeing.

But no signs of a delicate body of an Elf. That was good news, she might be alive!

Then I started to slowly carve my path thru the Gnoll patrolling the area. All my investment in blacksmith armor payed off, I was able to fight off three of them at a time. The moment I put on my mail coif I knew I did it well. Of course, I made it, it has to fit well. But the plans I acquired in the Barrens was specially detailed. The right mix of silver and bronze, the fitting with the expensive pristine leather, all add up. I won't even start to imagine what the Strange Dust I used during the procedure did to the ensemble. I felt as if my head was lighter, instead of heavy with all that metal. And it glows blue, a shade brighter than my boots! If you really concentrate you can see it, I swear.

Learning how to cast Consecration really paid off, too. 
You would think our Keep would provide some training discount for our brotherhood. Nope. And Hilda gave me a hard time, her title "The Breaker" is well deserved. But I knew I would need every bit of edge I could learn.

I needed a small respite. I wasn't tired or something, but the way that Tauren body was mangled, the weapons I just face in the hands of the Gnoll, their numbers patrolling the area, something wasn´t adding up.

I found that a good sharpening stone works wonders for the mind while sharpening a blade.
