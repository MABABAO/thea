I know I'd better start my way back to Bandarion Keep.

But Durotar is so beautiful! Sure it is a rugged land, but the savannas, the rolling hills, tigers and raptors and thunder lizards and boars! At first I was going south, but then I saw a beautiful sun starting his final stretch to the horizon and I couldn´t resist journeying into the sunset.

I never really noticed how I came to the Barrens. There was the biggest river I've ever seen and I crossed a bridge guarded by Orcs and Tauren.

Must be some kind of joke this land is called The Barrens.

Vast expanses everywhere you look. More rolling hills, bigger mountain ridges, more savannas, more exotic fauna. And some nasty native humanoids called Quilboars.

I noticed a tree silhouette far away in the horizon for the first time. But it can't be a tree, it would be so huge it would touch the sky!

I came to a Horde settlement called The Crossroads. Self-explanatory name.

I delivered a crate full of copper bars and I was enlisted in a rather exclusive society called Durotar Supply And Logistics. They have exquisite blacksmith plans that I just have to acquire. I am missing a helmet, too, and they happen to have plans for one.

Let's see what kind of help I can provide those guys.
