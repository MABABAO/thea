I am not procrastinating.

I don´t need excuses to stay beneath these open skies for a long time. I have been happy here.

I keep thinking of Silverpine. People live along those roads I was afraid to walk. They cannot all leave when the forest becomes too dangerous.

I want Wolfsbane. I do. But when I set out to prepare for it, I also wanted to stop running from the things I found there. Perhaps it is time to see how far I have come.

The thought still frightens me. I wish those people could feel as safe beneath their trees as I have felt beneath these open skies.

Somewhere in that forest, someone might be very glad to see a paladin.

I know I would have been.

One more thing, though, before I leave Kalimdor for the time being.

The children. 

The Horde have started this festival where we feed and clothe the children. I gave all my linen cloth and a lot of wool for the cause. And I also have a special quest, to help spread the festival, I need to visit the three capitals and perform silly dances and funny tasks to bring some laugh to the children, which I am all for it!

The next place on my list is Sen’jin Village. I have no idea where it is. Someone told me to take the road south, watch for signs, and ask along the way. Apparently I can’t miss it.  

What could be the closest thing to the Light than the ringing laugh of a child?

Undercity will be my last capital before Silverpine. Let the memory of radiant smiles be my shield.
