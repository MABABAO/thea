It is official, I couldn't deny it anymore, I made Orgrimmar my home for the time being and set my Hearthstone to the inn right beside the bank and the Auction House.

I need to train a lot more for my Wolfsbane. From time to time I will travel to Silverpine and test my mettle against a Son of Arugal. Didn't do even once since I run in terror the first time I saw it. I will judge if I am ready. Or ever.

As for my training, I usually place my trust on the good people of the Horde. Goblins, too, their needs usually involve lucrative ventures, so, why not. But otherwise, infiltrate this Alliance bastion and kill cannoniers killing our troops, hunt beasts for reagents, track lost people or patrols. 

I was even sent to a big mission inside the Wailing Caverns. What a labyrinth! 

I party with another 4 guys to join forces against the obstacles ahead. 

Early on, one remembered an important thing he had to do and left us. 

After a long time and lots of battles, one felt into an abyss and couldn't find a way back to us. The poor guy just sat there for sometime, cowering behind a rock because lots of snakes and snake-people were patrolling the area. After a while he just gave up and used his Hearthstone to teleport to his base.

Our tank was strong, I am very competent, our healer was amazing. So we pushed on, somewhat slower but steady. We got lost several times, that place was huge! How a forest can grow underground? Never mind, we persevere and found our way every time.

We didn't accomplish our main mission, though. 

That was a bad case of miscommunication. 

After a long stretch of searching, the tank made a turn and was excited we finally found our last checkpoint.

He immediately charged the giant Moss Beast. 

Meanwhile, our healer decided to replenish her mana pool to full before that boss encounter, which sounds very decent of her. 

The attack was savage, our tank was hit by two powerful consecutive strikes and the healer was too far away to cast an emergency spell. I would have cast it, I just didn't notice our healer was far away and thought I would just join the attack. 

Our tank died. 

I went to full attack using all my spells. One little explanation is in place. I usually don't go full power because we want the beasts to concentrate on our tank, who shouts and taunts, even if with all the fancy moves he is actually making less damage.

So the time to self control was over. I used everything, drank mana potions to use even more. Did some taunt myself but kept punching it. And then some more. 

But alas, in the end we all died. 

Thanks the Spirit Healer we could all come back to the dungeon. 

I was horrified to find myself a ghost but the guys told me to not worry, this was just an occupation hazard for dungeoning. 

They were very nonchalant about everything considering they were all ghosts, too, and we were standing beside a floating silvery, angel-looking, translucent ghost. This had happened to them before. and that was nothing to worry.

While they guided me back to the deep entrance of the Wailing Caverns, I was thinking about what death meant, now. Was I still a paladin? What about my powers, my training? I was too confused and was afraid to go astray, so I focused on them, which seemed very confident where they were going. How many times had they walked this way? 

The moment we crossed the dungeon threshold I was restored! I did a comprehensive inventory of my abilities. besides missing health and mana, everything seemed to be there. Holy Light, Flash of Light, Devotion Aura, blessing of Kings, everything there! What a scare! 

I asked them if there were limits to what has happened. How many times could I die that way? Were there penalties for recurring deaths? Apparently not, but the tank half-joked about the repairs being specially expensive for him, who always gets the brunt of the attacks, anyway. 

Are we immortals now? "As long as you have the will to go on,” the healer told me, “you might consider yourself immortal."
Hmm.
