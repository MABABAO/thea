Back to Orgrimar. With a purified Kor Gem safely in the bank.
Getting it was easy. Getting to it was not.

As soon as I left Orgrimar I went straight west. As long as I keep an eye open, there's no danger in some exploring. Some more copper ores, some more tin ores. Roads are for newbies.

I met some goblins on the way, they were all for the profit and not a bit friendly. Even Alliance types were fighting them. Which is why I steered away from them.

So, arriving at Ashenvale was a walk in the beautiful Barrens. 

Ashenvale in itself has some charms, but I miss the open horizons. The trees are huge and the canopies hide what otherwise would have been beautiful skies to watch.

Not that I would watch upwards that much, there was plenty to watch on the ground. Powerful beasts everywhere, definitely not a tamed land. There was a fortification at the entrance of Ashenvale and an outpost not far ahead. But that's it for Horde presence. 

The good news is that there was a flight point with ready to fly Wind Riders. I was in an scouting mission for the first of the three fundamental ingredients of the Wolfsbane and scouting ahead seemed wise. 

Warfare is expensive and exhausting, the military presence in Ashenvale expressed that with almost infinite calls for assistance. I wish I could have been more of help. Between powerful Elves and beasts, walking thru Ashenvale was not easy.

For the first time I really stick to the roads. And even so I was not safe from the nature's wrath. Apparently not even the Alliance, either. There seemed to be an implicit pact not written anywhere, we all stick to the roads and we don't attack each other in the roads. That was the way to go around these lands.

This was my longest walk ever. The Barrens might have been longer, but it was such a pleasant experience. Ashenvale was not.

Midway I saw in the distance Astranaar. The guys at Splintertree told me to steer away from it, it was an Alliance bastion. Problem was, it sat in the middle of the road. We can see the problem now, can we?

I had spent most of the journey watching the ground. Sitting there, I finally noticed the Wind Riders overhead.
It seems stick to the roads is also a good advice for flying travelers, noticing how they flew right up the road. 

Were they flying over Astranaar? No! They were slightly veering south when they approached the town. Off road was dangerous, but Astranaar was even more so. 

Carefully I traced the flight path and started my way around. I had to be patient in order to avoid huge bears and spiders. Just once I had to fight a giant spider, used everything I got, tank and spank style. I couldn't afford to move around, avoiding the spider's attacks could have draw attention from other beasts nearby. I carefully study its corpse and found a venom gland, similar to the other spider's I have found, with the obvious size difference. Spider meat for dinner and huge venom gland for the Auction House.  

That way I manage to cross the midway point of my journey thru Ashenvale. Yay! Midpoint!
