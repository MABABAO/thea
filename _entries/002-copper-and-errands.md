Survival requires coin, and my anxiety over my condition hasn't stopped the practical need to fill my pockets. Fortunately, Brill is understaffed and drowning in chores.
I spent the last few days running errands and stripping away the useless weight I was carrying - scraps of cloth armor and bizarre weapons I have no idea how to wield. Curiously, a mace feels right. The weight of it in my hand balances perfectly, matching a muscle memory my mind can't quite trace. The merchants here buy almost anything I scavenge, and the copper pieces are slowly turning into silver.
But Brill's resources are limited. There is a paladin trainer here, a stoic, quiet man who offers what small advice he can, but he urged me to seek real training elsewhere. I poked him several times, each time he answered me but after a while, he started giving me the same answers. I guess I was dismissed. I tried that on other inhabitants of Brill, some were really annoyed by my insistence. I had better stop doing that.
