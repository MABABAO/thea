So, worgen. That's where the Wolfsbane comes in. I wish it was a joke. 

Those blades started to appear by the time the werewolf curse started to spread in Silverpine. Some people were attacked by werewolves and started mutating. That explains what befell to Banon. 

Trevan told me that the worgen are a major threat to the citizens of Silverpine Forest. Despite their strength and resilience they can be killed, but there is one in particular that just doesn't ever seem to stay dead due to unknown magic. That's the one that attacked us, Old Fire-Eye, and he is the biggest and meanest of their kind. To put him down, we'll need a special kind of weapon. 

But the recipes are somewhat lost. Trevan knows the materials needed and while I look for them he will research the steps for the blade making. 

That research sounds time consuming, right?
Yeah, so is procuring the ingredients. At least I know where I can find Enchanted Silver Ingots, it is nearby, inside the Keep just south of the Sepulcher. I met the Son of Arugal in the forest, I do not fancy to meet the actual Arugal, whomever he is.

Trevan included in his list a special Oak Lumber to be acquired from goblins in the Deadmines and a Kor Gem from the Naga in Blackfathom Deeps. I don't know half the words in that sentence.
