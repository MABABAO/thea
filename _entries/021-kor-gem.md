Now comes the great battle for the magical gem!
Sorry to disappoint you, the second half of my journey thru Ashenvale was just boring, really boring.

Not that it was uneventful. Oh no. 

I left Astranaar behind, but the bears and the wolves and the spiders were even more terrible as I approached the heart of Ashenvale. Sticking to the roads was not a sure thing anymore. It was all about waiting for the wild beasts to wander away from the road so I could continue to move on. Several times I just run for it, unashamed.

There were other small Alliance settlements nearby, too, thankfully not that close to the road.

Then, far away in the distance I notice a spiked head of a watch tower. Horde!

No matter how hard I tried, I couldn't find a safe path to it from the road I was on. 

If it wasn´t too dangerous, I would have crawled to it, but I cannot crawl as fast as I can run. So I run. 

Should have been funny for an outsider to watch an undead followed by a group of wolves, two big bears and a couple of giant spider throwing nets and spitting venom.

And that's how I arrived at Zoram'gar outpost, in the Zoram Strand. The Horde troops watching me were really having a blast.
I know, not exactly the heroic image.

After I finished removing the poisons, yes, plural, healed myself and had a good drink I finally started walking around and meeting the good people. 

They had a windrider flight master, too! Good, I wouldn't need to face Ashenvale's road anymore!

So I met Ulric Frostveil. He was still chuckling when I asked about Naga and Kor Gem. 
"A Forsaken paladin... here in Ashenvale? Amusing."
"Yeah, I cannot stop laughing" I answered.
"So you seek a Kor Gem? Then you must head north to the ruins known as Blackfathom Deeps. The Naga who still use the gems are down below." Do not go alone, paladin.

So I went alone.

Ulric explained these gems are employed to empower magical weapons and implements by the paladins of the Alliance. Whys should it be any different for a paladin like me? But the Naga used only corrupted gems for their rituals. He asked me to bring one of those.

I fought some Naga on my way to the Blackfathom Deep, I fought some more in the entrance of the ruins, I fought yet some more inside the ruins. When I was preparing myself to enter its dungeons, one particularly vicious Naga witch obsequiously complied and dropped a Corrupted Gem. 

Back at Zoram'gar, apothecary Ulric performed a simple spell to purify it. But not before offering a handsome amount of gold to me to part with that gem. He told me he needed a corrupt gem "for academic purposes." Yeah, right. 

And I took the fastest wind rider back to Orgrimmar.
