Bandarion Keep! I made it!
Again I found myself sleepless, but there are good reasons for that! Paladins! Everywhere! High-level paladins, at that. The paladin trainer, Hilda The Breaker, was actually training paladins to fight! I couldn't stop at one place; I had to see it all. The blacksmith is actually wearing plate armor! The stable actually has holy paladin mounts! I couldn't wait to talk to the Keep commander.

I made my way all the way up the Keep, and then I saw them: Commander Danitha Morr and Leonid Barthalomew The Revered. His two-handed axe was huge and shiny! Her two-handed mace was all silver and gold - I could feel a radiating... pressure? I don't know, something emanating from her. They both looked unmistakably undead, but they were not wearing rags. Their presence was commanding. I don't think the mundane powers of the world could even touch them.

They signaled to me to come closer. I was welcomed, and they were directly including me in their conversation! I know, I feel like a rabid fan. All that because I am a newly recruited paladin?! 
I had expected orders. Instead, he told me about his friends.
I stopped looking at his axe.

He spoke to me of the past, recounting how he and Danitha had fought side-by-side against the Scourge to defend the Undercity when Naxxramas attacked. They lost many good friends that day, including Garek Bandarion - the hero this keep was named after. Apparently, Garek might just have been the first of the Forsaken to rediscover how to wield the Light as a paladin. 

I had so many questions! I hardly dared ask one. Every answer might lead to something else everyone here knew except me.
But neither of them seemed to mind that I was still standing there, listening.

Lord Barthalomew said he felt deeply encouraged to see Garek's legacy and potential living on through Commander Danitha and her cohort, though he wished that the rest of the Forsaken shared those feelings. He planned to stay at the keep for a little while to speak with more friends, but mentioned he'd be returning to Light's Hope Chapel in the Eastern Plaguelands soon enough. 

Then he invited me to visit him at Light’s Hope Chapel.
Me. If I happened to be passing.
I tried to answer as though that were an ordinary thing to be asked.

If my jaw were any less firmly attached, it would have dropped.

I was put to work almost immediately. More Murlocs - funny guys. There was plenty of honorable work, too: infiltration, fighting Scarlet Crusaders, recovering artifacts, and helping to maintain the Keep. 

Commander Danitha sent me downstairs to meet a deathguard who had petitioned the Keep for aid. “Show him and the Forsaken what a true paladin can do.”

Deathguard Billmuth. Poor guy. He looked as though he expected one sneeze from the powerful paladins around him to reduce him to ash. Yet he had come here asking for help.

He and his partner had been forced to flee during a dangerous mission. Falgon might have stayed behind to save his life. Billmuth had tried to find a priest, but none was available, and whoever went looking would need to survive the same dangers that had driven them out.

So he was asking me.

I stopped finding his nervousness quite so funny. He wanted his partner back, and whatever reservations he had about us, he wasn't letting them stand in the way.

The least I could do was try to find Falgon. I might need to resurrect him, though I have no idea how I will manage it.

I thought I could wait until morning. I don’t think I can.
